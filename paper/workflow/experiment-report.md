## Evidence limits / 证据边界

One US survivor-selected equity universe. No independent market confirmation, no full raw-minute reconstruction, no degree-conditioned null test, no faithful Sig-GSPHAR reproduction. The scalar permutation identity and residualization are established tools, not new theorems. Legacy CGI and NTD use different graph information timing and must not be treated as a matched before/after improvement. Negative, zero-weight and placebo outcomes remain in the archive.
