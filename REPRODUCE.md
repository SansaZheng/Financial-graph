# Reproduction entry points

Use the exact install, authentication, selective-download, CQ execution and upload commands in [README.md](README.md). The authoritative asset routing is [assets/manifest.json](assets/manifest.json); the rolling development split definition is [configs/rolling-development.json](configs/rolling-development.json).

New checkouts do not contain datasets or generated predictions. Do not run historical job wrappers that assume `evidence/` exists inside Git. Those wrappers/specs remain as code-history provenance for old pinned commits, not the default entry point.

After private derived assets are uploaded and pinned, `scripts/run_experiment.py` verifies their hashes and passes explicit data/daily paths into the unchanged scientific evaluators. The modern evaluator now accepts `--daily`; model/hyperparameter behavior is unchanged. Existing results were generated by the older recorded commits, not retrospectively relabeled as new runs.

Raw minute data is reused at the existing HF revision. Full raw checksum and independent backup receipts remain in `maintenance/raw-release-verification.json`; do not download the11GB source to the Mac for routine development. Streaming checks are bounded range reads, not full-file hash verification.

The experimental target remains unmet: all three NTD seeds were worse than the validation-selected strong control. Storage tests and a clean environment check do not establish scientific superiority.
